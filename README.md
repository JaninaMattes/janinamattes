### Hi, I'm Janina 👋

I'm currently building an AI agent that reads financial reports, links what it finds into a
knowledge graph, and shows the exact page behind every answer. Before that, I spent my MSc at
LMU Munich looking inside image generation models, to understand what they learn and to steer
what they create.

What I care about most: **AI you can check**, whose behaviour is reliable, testable and
explainable.

---

**Currently**

- Building [VerityGraph-RAG](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/VerityGraph-RAG), for questions
  that span several pages of a report, where plain vector search falls short
- Working on autonomous driving at [Munich Motorsport](https://github.com/munichmotorsport):
  EKF SLAM, which builds a map and locates the car in it at the same time, with ROS 2
- Next: testing LLM pipelines the way we test software, with regression tests that catch an
  answer getting worse, and running them on Kubernetes

---

**Research**

Image generators learn rich internal representations, but they keep them to themselves. In my
thesis, [NoiseGuided-SmoothLFM](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/NoiseGuided-SmoothLFM), I
trained a model to find interpretable codes inside a flow matching model's latent space, then
used those codes to guide what it generates.

---

**Other projects**

- [Autonomous-Explorer-Drone](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/Autonomous-Explorer-Drone): a
  simulated quadrotor that learns to take off and hold stable flight, trained with my own PPO
  implementation in PyTorch
- [Fudge-Cooking-App](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/Fudge-Cooking-App): keeps track of what
  is in your kitchen and its use-by dates, and suggests recipes that use food up before it goes
  off (Android and Spring Boot)
