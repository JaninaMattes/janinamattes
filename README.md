### Hi, I'm Janina 👋

I'm a software engineer in Sydney working on AI systems, from LLM retrieval and agents to
computer vision and generative models. I care most about **trustworthy AI**: understanding how
models represent what they learn, and making their behaviour reliable, testable and explainable.

---

**Currently**

- Building [VerityGraph-RAG](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/VerityGraph-RAG), an agentic
  GraphRAG system that answers questions over financial reports and cites the source page for
  every answer
- Working on autonomous driving and EKF SLAM with [ROS 2](https://github.com/munichmotorsport)
- Next: regression testing and reliability for LLM pipelines, and deploying them on Kubernetes

---

**Research**

[NoiseGuided-SmoothLFM](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/NoiseGuided-SmoothLFM), my MSc thesis
at LMU Munich, looks inside flow matching image models: it learns interpretable codes in the
model's latent space and uses them to guide and control what the model generates.

---

**Other projects**

- [Autonomous-Explorer-Drone](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/Autonomous-Explorer-Drone): a
  quadrotor that learns to fly with reinforcement learning (PPO in PyTorch and PyBullet)
- [Fudge-Cooking-App](https://github.com/JaninaMattes/Fudge-Cooking-App): an Android
  application with a Spring Boot backend that tracks what you use and suggests recipes for the ingredients you have
